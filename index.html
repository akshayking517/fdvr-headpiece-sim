Does a battery‑less headpiece work?

The near-term headpiece paints the image directly onto your retina with a tiny projector, instead of showing it on a screen and lens stack, and draws its power from the room, over the air. The room can safely deliver about 20 mW. The essay gives a range, not a number, for most of what the headpiece burns. So here are 5,000 headpieces, each built from a random point in every one of those ranges.

Model: head-side load is receive power (delivered bit rate × energy per bit) plus projector power (light ÷ wall-plug efficiency) plus tracking plus conversion loss. Room delivery is field strength × capture area × harvesting efficiency. The thermal ceiling is 94.8 W/m² of contact (84.8 W/m² if the device blocks all heat loss to room air), from a four-layer tissue heat-transfer model held to a 1 °C rise.

The essay's endgame, writing straight to the visual cortex, uses the same room node and power envelope with a different last component. That component doesn't exist yet and isn't modelled here.
